## New evidence and its limits

The [official C310 page](https://schaltbau.com/en/product/contactors/c310/)
names both configurations and identifies the C310A operating voltage as 1,000 V.
Its 1,500 V insulation headline is not used as an operating-voltage rating.
The specifications table gives 300 A with 185 mm² conductors at 70°C and 500 A
with two 150 mm² conductors at 40°C. The table does not explicitly identify the
temperature reference, so that remains unstated.

The page's feature prose instead names C360 and describes a different 500 A
condition. That discrepancy is retained for review; the candidates quote only
the C310 specifications table. No breaking capacity, coil voltage, certification
or release date is inferred. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403, and the old
catalogue URL and current linked catalogue endpoint returned HTML rather than a
PDF. No original-content hash or PDF revision is claimed for this new source.
