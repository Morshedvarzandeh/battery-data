The three still being built

The same cell. The same page.
Both numbers are true.

Samsung SDI INR21700-50E is published with two capacities. Neither is wrong. They were measured at different rates, and a column called capacity_mah keeps one of them and quietly loses the other.

Capacity (standard)
4,900mAh
0.2 C25 °Cto 4.2 V2.5 V cut-offon dischargeC referred to 4.9 Ah
Capacity (rated)
4,753mAh
1 C25 °Cto 4.2 V2.5 V cut-offon dischargeC referred to 4.9 Ah

Same document, same page. The difference is the rate.

Honest about the gaps

50% of these values arrive
without their conditions.

300 of the 600 values in this library were published without the rate, the temperature or the cut-off they were taken at. That is not a flaw in the library — it is what the documents say. Each of those values is marked, quantity by quantity, rather than completed with a plausible guess.

Specifications are quoted from manufacturers’ own published documents and remain the property of their publishers. No datasheet is redistributed here: each value is stored with the sentence it was read from, a link to the document and the date it was retrieved.

Where a document did not state the rate, the temperature or the cut‑off a value was measured at, this site says so rather than filling the gap. Nothing on these pages is a manufacturer’s endorsement, and nothing here is a substitute for the current datasheet.
