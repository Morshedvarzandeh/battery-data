An equivalent-circuit model: open-circuit voltage against state of charge for a flat-plateau chemistry, scaled to this cell’s published voltages, less the current through its internal resistance, which rises as it gets colder. Heating is adiabatic at 1000 J/(kg·K). This cell’s document did not state: charge limit 3.65 V; cut-off 2.5 V; internal resistance 4 mΩ. It will not match a cycler. It is here to show which way the numbers move, and how far. Read the model.

Where a document did not state the rate, the temperature or the cut‑off a value was measured at, this site says so rather than filling the gap. Nothing on these pages is a manufacturer’s endorsement, and nothing here is a substitute for the current datasheet.
