### Scope and evidence limits

- **CATL — First-generation sodium-ion battery:** CATL platform unveiling; not the invention of sodium-ion chemistry, a catalog cell release, or proof of commercial shipment.
- **CATL — Shenxing, first generation:** Named fast-charge LFP family. No release date is assigned to the unrelated CATL 302Ah or 314Ah catalog cells.
- **CATL — Naxtra sodium-ion battery:** Launch of a named family. Promotional mass-production wording does not establish first customer shipment; retain the later production plan separately.
- **CATL — Shenxing, second generation:** Second-generation family launch, not a new release date for every LFP cell.
- **CATL — Shenxing, third generation:** Third-generation family unveiling. This date does not establish commercial availability of an orderable cell SKU.
- **CATL — Naxtra full-scale production plan:** The April 2026 article forecasts full-scale mass production. This is a plan, not proof of shipment, and remains a plan even after its target year passes.
- **CALB — ZHIJIU 392Ah / 588Ah / 661Ah long-cycle ESS family:** Event date is April 1; the article date is April 2. Applies only to the three named capacity versions; does not date the CALB 684Ah cell or imply exact cathode chemistry.
- **HiTHIUM — ∞Cell N162Ah:** Named product announcement; its numeric candidate remains subject to the separate observation/identity review. Sampling is distinct from volume deliveries.
- **HiTHIUM — ∞Cell N162Ah production plan:** Historical plan in the December 2024 article. Completion has not been established by this source; no quarter start or end day is fabricated.
- **WeLion — Second-generation semi-solid-state technology roadmap:** Official European distributor roadmap, not verified completion or a release date for the existing SHP270/SHP320 models. Semi-solid is not all-solid-state.
- **WeLion — Third-generation fully solid-state technology roadmap:** Official European distributor roadmap for a future generation; not a currently released cell. The page has no recorded publication date.
